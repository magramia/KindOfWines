Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026

The service

KindOfWines provides sommelier recommendations based on a photo of the wine list uploaded by the user. Each consultation costs €2.99 and includes a podium of 3 selected wines, with the option to request an additional fourth suggestion.

Nature of the service

KindOfWines is a service created and curated by M.Grace, an Italian sommelier, who personally defined the selection criteria (the Classic, the Hidden One, the Unexpected) and the style guidelines that the system follows. Recommendations are generated using artificial intelligence and may contain inaccuracies, especially in the case of unclear photos.

Refund policy

If the system is unable to correctly read the wine list provided and explicitly notifies the user, the user may upload a new photo at no additional cost within the same session. If, despite a readable photo, the service fails to provide a result due to a technical error, the user may request a refund by writing to kali62spera@yahoo.it, including a description of the issue.

No subscription

KindOfWines does not require account creation or any subscription. Each payment is a one-time charge related solely to the consultation requested at that time.

Limitation of liability

The wine suggestions provided are based on subjective preferences and do not guarantee the availability of the recommended product at the venue where the user is located, nor its suitability for specific dietary needs, allergies, or interactions with medication. The user is responsible for final verification before consumption.

Privacy

Uploaded photos are used exclusively to generate the requested consultation and are not permanently stored or shared with third parties for purposes other than processing the service.

Contact

For questions, refunds, or reports: kali62spera@yahoo.it
